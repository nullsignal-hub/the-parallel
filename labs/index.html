The cell type you study, printed from the connectome

Search for a cell type in the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome of the adult male Drosophila. The poster overlays every reconstructed neuron of that type, drawn from the published skeletons in one of five styles.

  • What arrivesA poster, 18×24 in or 24×36 in, printed on matte paper and shipped to you. Rendered at 300 dpi from the skeletons, frontal view.
  • On the sheetThe type name, the number of neurons drawn, an optional one-line caption of yours, and the dataset credit (CC BY 4.0).
  • DataMaleCNS v1.0 (FlyEM, HHMI-Janelia; Cambridge Connectomics Group; Google Research). Cell types are the dataset's own annotations. Up to 250 neurons of the type are drawn.
